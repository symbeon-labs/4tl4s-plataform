# ADR-0003 — Govern research-to-development transfer

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decision date:** 2026-11-01
- **Decision owner:** Symbeon Labs platform direction
- **Scope:** Applied Research and Product Development

## Context

Research can produce promising hypotheses, methods, models, or prototypes, but research evidence does not by itself establish engineering feasibility, product readiness, ownership rights, or permission to commercialize.

## Decision

Transfer from Applied Research to Product Development requires a documented review. The review must capture the source research, evidence and reproducibility, limitations, maturity, technical and operational risks, IP provenance and third-party restrictions, receiving owner, scope, and acceptance criteria.

The transfer decision may be accepted, conditionally accepted, deferred, or rejected. The receiving development initiative remains separately identified and governed.

## Consequences

- Research completion and product readiness remain distinct.
- Negative and inconclusive research outcomes remain valid records.
- Engineering assumptions and validation tasks are explicit at intake.
- Transfer decisions become auditable and reversible through superseding decisions where needed.
