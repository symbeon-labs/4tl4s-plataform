# Commercial Operations

## Mission

Commercial Operations coordinates the relationship between Symbeon and its clients or market: opportunities, qualification, proposals, contracts, obligations, project economics, delivery, and acceptance.

## Core workflows

### Opportunity
Capture source, organization, contacts, need, qualification, estimated value, probability, next action, owner, and decision history. Forecasts are estimates, not booked revenue.

### Proposal
Record scope, assumptions, exclusions, deliverables, schedule, pricing, payment terms, validity, risks, and approval status. A draft proposal is not a commitment.

### Contract and obligations
Store controlled references to executed agreements, amendments, parties, effective dates, scope, milestones, payment terms, confidentiality, IP clauses, change control, acceptance, termination, and obligations. The authoritative signed document remains the legal source of truth.

### Client project
Create a bounded project linked to the governing contract and obligations. Record scope, accountable owner, work packages, milestones, deliverables, dependencies, risks, budget, and change requests.

### Delivery and acceptance
Track internal review, delivery event, client feedback, acceptance or rejection, remediation, and closure. Delivery is not automatically acceptance or payment.

## Financial visibility

The platform may track estimates, approved budgets, actual costs, time, invoicing references, receivables, and margin calculations. Financial records must distinguish forecast, approved, invoiced, received, and reconciled amounts. Accounting and tax records remain subject to the authoritative financial system and professional controls.

## Client isolation

Client data and documents are accessible only to authorized engagement participants. Cross-client reporting must use approved aggregation and avoid disclosing confidential information. Reusable Symbeon IP and client-owned assets must be classified and permissioned separately.

## Commercial control gates

Human approval is required for proposals sent externally, contract execution, pricing exceptions, scope changes, expenditure commitments, invoices/credits where applicable, IP licensing or assignment, and final acceptance/closure.

## First operational use

The initial internal validation should use Symbeon's own commercial workflow before introducing external client workspaces. New client engagements should be onboarded only after access boundaries, contract references, and data-handling rules are established.
