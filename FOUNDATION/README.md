# Foundation — 4TL4S Operating Model

## Purpose

4TL4S is the operational coordination layer for Symbeon Labs. Its purpose is to organize initiatives, responsibilities, resources, evidence, decisions, and commitments while preserving the autonomy of the underlying projects.

## Operating model

The platform recognizes four primary initiative classes:

| Class | Primary question | Expected outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Applied Research | What can we establish through investigation? | Validated or rejected hypothesis, method, finding, or research artifact |
| Product Development | What should we engineer and make usable? | Tested software, platform, component, or technical system |
| Artifact Production | What artifact must be produced and controlled? | Versioned, reviewed, accepted artifact |
| Commercial Project | What have we committed to a client or market? | Contractually scoped delivery and recorded acceptance |

A commercial project may contain development and artifact-production work packages. It remains commercially governed while its technical work is classified and tracked in its appropriate domain.

## Lifecycle

A common high-level lifecycle is:

1. **Intake** — register the initiative, sponsor, purpose, primary class, and initial constraints.
2. **Qualification** — assess strategic relevance, feasibility, capacity, risk, funding, and ownership.
3. **Authorization** — approve a bounded scope, accountable owner, resources, and permissions.
4. **Execution** — conduct research, engineering, production, or commercial work under its domain rules.
5. **Review** — evaluate evidence, quality, scope, risks, and outcomes.
6. **Closure or transition** — close, continue, transfer, publish, commercialize, or archive with a recorded decision.

Not every initiative follows an identical sequence. Domain-specific lifecycle rules may add gates, but must not silently bypass shared governance.

## Research-to-development transfer

A research output may be proposed for engineering only through a documented transfer review. The review records:

- source research initiative and version;
- hypothesis, findings, and supporting evidence;
- limitations, unresolved questions, and reproducibility status;
- technology maturity and assumptions;
- IP provenance, contributor rights, and third-party constraints;
- proposed receiving initiative, scope, owner, and acceptance criteria;
- decision: accepted, conditionally accepted, deferred, or rejected.

Transfer is not automatic ownership assignment, proof of product readiness, or permission to disclose or commercialize.

## Cross-domain relationships

Initiatives may be related through explicit relationship types such as:
- informs;
- derived-from;
- validates;
- implements;
- produces;
- depends-on;
- contracted-through;
- delivers-to.

Relationships must preserve the identity, owner, access policy, and lifecycle of each initiative.

## Non-goals

4TL4S is not intended to:
- replace GitHub, source-control workflows, or project-specific engineering systems;
- centralize every client document without access and retention rules;
- infer contractual rights from technical contribution;
- convert every research result into a product;
- grant autonomous agents unrestricted authority;
- present unverified claims as established facts.

## Foundational rule

**Coordination does not imply ownership; visibility does not imply access; evidence does not imply approval; and a recorded decision does not imply execution.**
